1. EXECUTIVE ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version 1.0.11 introduces a pure, 100% optional declarative metadata &
introspection layer (`RegionContentRegistry`) paired with server-authoritative,
region-scoped tab-completion for `/locate region <region> [biome|structure]`:

1. Optional Region Content Metadata & Inter-Addon Introspection (`RegionContentRegistry` & `RegionalApi`):
   - Addons can optionally declare which biome IDs, biome tags, structure IDs,
     and structure tags belong to a region via `RegionalApi.getInstance()`:
     * `registerRegionBiome(regionId, biomeId)` / `registerRegionBiomes(...)`
     * `registerRegionBiomeTag(regionId, biomeTag)` / `registerRegionBiomeTags(...)`
     * `registerRegionStructure(regionId, structureId)` / `registerRegionStructures(...)`
     * `registerRegionStructureTag(regionId, structureTag)` / `registerRegionStructureTags(...)`
   - Any third-party mod (map, quest, compass, or bridge addon) can introspect
     registered region content purely through `RegionalApi` without depending
     on the registering addon:
     * `getRegisteredRegionBiomes(regionId)` (automatically includes `BiomeMaskRule` IDs)
     * `getRegisteredRegionBiomeTags(regionId)` / `hasRegisteredRegionBiomes(regionId)`
     * `getRegisteredRegionStructures(regionId)` / `getRegisteredRegionStructureTags(regionId)`
     * `hasRegisteredRegionStructures(regionId)`
     * `isBiomeRegisteredForRegion(regionId, biomeId | Holder<Biome>)`
     * `isStructureRegisteredForRegion(regionId, structureId | Holder<Structure>)`
   - Preserves 100% backward compatibility with existing `RegionDefinition`
     constructors and incurs zero overhead on worldgen/chunk hot paths.

2. Region-Scoped Server-Authoritative Tab-Completion (`LocateRegionCommand`):
   - Structure Suggestions (`suggestStructuresForRegion`):
     * If `<region>` has registered structures or structure tags, tab-completion
       exclusively suggests those entries (including expanded members of
       registered `#tags`), keeping suggestions strictly scoped to that region.
     * If `<region>` has no structure registrations, tab-completion automatically
       falls back to the global `Registries.STRUCTURE` registry and tags.
   - Biome Suggestions (`suggestBiomesForRegion`):
     * If `<region>` has explicit biome/tag registrations OR a dual
       `BiomeMaskRule` (both surface and cave overrides set), tab-completion
       exclusively suggests the region's registered/masked biomes and tags.
     * Otherwise (no explicit registrations and no dual mask), tab-completion
       automatically falls back to the global `Registries.BIOME` registry and tags.
   - Works identically across NeoForge, Fabric, and Forge in both Singleplayer
     and Dedicated Server multiplayer via Brigadier's native `ask_server`
     suggestion protocol with zero extra network packets.

3. Vanilla-Exact Full-Chunk Structure Discovery (`RegionSpatialLocator`):
   - Replaced artificial candidate caps (`128`), chunk-load caps (`256`), and
     main-thread time truncation (`500 ms`) with synchronous, ring-by-ring
     (`r = 0 .. 100`) chunk verification mirroring vanilla Minecraft's
     `ChunkGenerator#findNearestMapStructure` + O(1) `RegionalApi#getRegionRawId`
     pre-filtering and ring-level early exit.
   - Eliminates false negatives for rare biome-constrained structures (e.g.,
     `minecraft:village_desert`, `minecraft:woodland_mansion`, modded structures)
     across repeating regions, unique QuadTree regions, and concentric rings.
