1. EXECUTIVE ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version 1.0.8 resolves the server startup lifecycle and spawn-chunk caching race
condition on NeoForge and Forge:

1. Two-Phase Server Startup Lifecycle (Pre-World-Load Freeze + Post-Level-Create SavedData Sync):
   On NeoForge and Forge, `ServerStartingEvent` is posted AFTER `MinecraftServer#loadLevel()`
   and `prepareLevels()` have already generated and loaded all spawn chunks. In v1.0.7,
   binding `freezeAndIndex` to `ServerStartingEvent` meant that during initial spawn chunk
   generation/loading, `RegionRegistry.isFrozen()` was `false` and `activeWorldSeed` was `0L`,
   causing `VoronoiEngine2D` to fall back to `0` (`regionalapi:wilds`) and `BiomeMaskEvaluationHelper`
   to skip 3D biome overrides on spawn chunks.
   Resolved across all three loaders (`:neoforge`, `:fabric`, `:forge`) using a two-phase lifecycle:
   - Phase 1 (Before `loadLevel()`): `ServerAboutToStartEvent` (NeoForge/Forge) and
     `ServerLifecycleEvents.SERVER_STARTING` (Fabric) compute and lock the authoritative salted
     regional seed and execute `RegionRegistry.getInstance().freezeAndIndex(server)` BEFORE
     any world or spawn chunk is created or loaded.
   - Phase 2 (After `loadLevel()`): `ServerStartingEvent` (NeoForge/Forge) and
     `ServerLifecycleEvents.SERVER_STARTED` (Fabric) execute
     `RegionRegistry.getInstance().syncUniqueRegionsWithOverworld(server)` once
     `server.overworld()` and its `DimensionDataStorage` are initialized.

2. Epoch-Validated Chunk Duck Interface (`MixinChunkAccess` / `IRegionChunkData`):
   Added `@Unique private long regional$populatedEpoch` to `MixinChunkAccess` verified against
   `ThreadLocalRegionCache.getCurrentEpoch()` inside `regional$isInitialized()`.
   - Any chunk queried prior to `RegionRegistry.isFrozen()` or with an unset seed is never
     locked as initialized in `MixinChunkAccess`, `ChunkRegionAttachment`, `MixinChunkSerializer`,
     or `ThreadLocalRegionCache`.
   - Whenever `CACHE_EPOCH` increments (such as on `freezeAndIndex`, `setVoronoiCellShift`,
     `configureGenerationMode`, or `syncUniqueRegionsWithOverworld`), all in-memory chunks
     automatically invalidate their cached `IRegionChunkData` in O(1) time without iterating chunks,
     and existing worlds whose spawn chunks were previously saved as `regionalapi:wilds` in v1.0.7
     are automatically healed on chunk read.
